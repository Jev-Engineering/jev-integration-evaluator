# Frozen experiment plan

Record study ID, candidate/source/rubric/model/policy hashes and task-dataset version. Identify the causal question, assignment/pairing unit, independent clusters, primary endpoint, minimum useful effect, safety/resource/false-block limits and fixed stopping rule. State how failed and timed-out scheduled tasks remain in the denominator.

Describe baseline and each ablation, randomization/counterbalancing, isolated task state, seed schedule, task hashes, label provenance and independence, calibration split, untouched test set and leakage controls. Record which outcomes are decision accuracy versus actual task success.

Predeclare paired effect and uncertainty methods, Bayesian prior/support assumptions, subgroup analysis, multiplicity adjustment, Pareto tradeoffs, cost/price provenance, cache revalidation and break-even sensitivity. Do not choose thresholds or questions on the holdout.

Document authorized execution/egress scope, immediate rollback/off control and host-owned policy. Record completed/failed/not-run tests. A study result is not permission to deploy; a no-value conclusion is valid.
